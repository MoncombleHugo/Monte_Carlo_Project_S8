In the ST7 specific course : discrete-time stochastic calculus, option
pricing, binomial tree.
The price of a European call option in a binomial tree can be
computed by backward recursion...but the formula at t = 0 is not so
nice.
Taking the continuous time limit, we get to the famous
Black-Scholes model, where the price of a European option is in a
closed form. The price can also be written as an integral.
The Monte-Carlo method, a non-probabilistic application of the
Strong Law of Large Numbers and the Central Limit Theorem
(CLT), enables the approximate calculation of integrals or the
expectation of random variables.
All banks use calculators based on this method.
Tutor Objective Background
Objective II
Whatever the confidence level chosen for the precision of a result
obtained by the Monte Carlo method, the amplitude of the
asymptotic interval provided by the CLT is proportional to the
standard deviation.
To reduce this standard deviation, several methods are available,
depending on the properties of the function to be integrated. Bearing
in mind that these heuristics are not general methods, but are
applied on a case-by-case basis, the aim of this project is to study
the application of some of them to the computation of option prices.
All these methods will first be tested on one-dimensional European
options to assess their reliability, since a closed-form formula exists,
and then on basket options.
A sensitivity study will also be carried out as a function of the
parameters of the Black-Scholes model.
All the results will be presented in a user-friendly pricer format,
including comparisons of the different methods and a sensitivity
study.
Project title : Variance reduction methods for Monte Carlo option
pricing.
Project goal :
Understand the Monte Carlo method and the variance reduction
methods.
Apply them to the computation of European price options first in
dimension one and then for basket options.
Provide a sensitivity study as a function of the parameters of the
Black-Scholes model.
Deliverables : user-friendly pricer, code, technical report with
numerical results, oral presentation.
